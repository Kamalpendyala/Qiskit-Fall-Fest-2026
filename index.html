01 / Quantum starts here

01 — The invitation

A festival for the quantum curious.

Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 brings students, builders, researchers, and first-time explorers together at MGIT Hyderabad — organized by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering in collaboration with IBM Quantum.

MGIT is one of roughly 200 institutions selected from about 900 applicants worldwide to host the 2026 edition, and one of only two in Telangana, alongside IIT Hyderabad. This year marks A Decade of Quantum Computing on the Cloud — ten years since IBM opened the world's first cloud-accessible quantum computer.

02 — Follow the signal

Programme in formation.

12–14 October 2026, 10:00 AM–3:00 PM. A three-day progression: learn from experts → build and run quantum programs → interact with the wider quantum ecosystem.

  1. 01
    12 Oct · Day 1

    Expert Talks & Keynotes

    Insights from experts across industry and academia covering the basics of quantum computing, the quantum landscape, applications, research directions, and career opportunities.

  2. 02
    13 Oct · Day 2

    Hands-on Qiskit Workshop

    A practical introduction to quantum computing and Qiskit — build and run real quantum circuits and programs.

  3. 03
    14 Oct · Day 3

    Quantum Community Interaction

    An open space for students, researchers, faculty, professionals, and enthusiasts to ask questions, exchange ideas, and build connections.
