01 / Quantum starts here

01 — The invitation

A festival for the quantum curious.

Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 brings students, builders, researchers, and first-time explorers together at MGIT Hyderabad — organized by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering in collaboration with IBM Quantum.

MGIT is one of roughly 200 institutions selected from about 900 applicants worldwide to host the 2026 edition, and one of only two in Telangana, alongside IIT Hyderabad. This year marks A Decade of Quantum Computing on the Cloud — ten years since IBM opened the world's first cloud-accessible quantum computer.

04 — A decade on the cloud

Ten years, one qubit at a time.

Qiskit Fall Fest turns ten. Here's the flight path that brought quantum computing from a five-qubit experiment to a hands-on festival at MGIT.

  1. 2016

    The first quantum computer goes live on the cloud — five qubits, open to everyone.

  2. 2019

    IBM Quantum System One, the first integrated quantum computer, comes online.

  3. 2021

    The 127-qubit Eagle processor pushes devices toward utility scale.

  4. 2023

    The first IBM quantum computer is installed at a hospital — Cleveland Clinic.

  5. 2024

    Japan's first IBM Quantum System Two debuts at RIKEN.

  6. 2026

    Qiskit Fall Fest arrives at MGIT, Hyderabad.

    You are here

07 — Get quantum-ready

Set up before you arrive.

Day 2 is hands-on. Install Qiskit and the IBM Quantum tools ahead of time so you spend the workshop building circuits, not fighting installs.

  1. 01

    Install Python 3.11+ and create a virtual environment.

  2. 02

    Install Qiskit and Qiskit IBM Runtime with pip.

  3. 03

    Create a free IBM Quantum account and generate an API key.

  4. 04

    Run a test circuit locally, or straight from IBM Quantum Composer in the browser.
