01 — The invitation

A festival for the quantum curious.

Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 brings students, builders, researchers, and first-time explorers together at MGIT Hyderabad for a hands-on entry into quantum technologies.

Come for the ideas that bend intuition. Leave with the tools, people, and confidence to keep asking better questions.

02 — Follow the signal

Programme in formation.

12–14 October 2026, 9:00 AM–4:00 PM. A three-day progression: learn from experts → build and run quantum programs → interact with the wider quantum ecosystem.

  1. 01
    12 Oct · Day 1

    Expert Lectures

    Sessions by eminent people from industry and academia covering the basics of quantum computing, the quantum landscape, current developments, applications, research directions, and careers.

  2. 02
    13 Oct · Day 2

    Hands-on Qiskit Workshop

    Participants learn Qiskit fundamentals, build quantum circuits, and execute quantum programs through guided practical work.

  3. 03
    14 Oct · Day 3

    Community Interaction

    Companies, students, researchers, faculty, professionals, and quantum enthusiasts interact, ask questions, exchange ideas, and build connections.
